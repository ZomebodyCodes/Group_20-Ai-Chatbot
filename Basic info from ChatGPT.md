- **Hardrada** commonly translated as "Hard Ruler" or "Harsh Ruler", so he gained reputation as a forceful & uncompromising leader. 
- At 15 years old, fought alongside "Saint Olaf" at the Battle of Stiklestad in 1030
- Olaf was killed during the battle, and Harald was wounded
- Harald fled Norway to avoid capture after the defeat
- After fleeing, he reached the **Byzantine Empire**, where he served in the **Varangian Guard**
- He spent time in the lands of the **Rus**, serving under **Prince Yaroslav the Wise**
- He gained considerable wealth, experience and military reputation through his services
- Eventually returned to Scandinavia with enough resources and influence to pursue the Norwegian throne
- Returned to Norway ~1045
- Formed an agreement with **King Magnus the Good**, his nephew
- In 1046, Harald became co-ruler alongside Magnus
- Magnus died in 1047, allowing for Harald to become the sole ruler of Norway
